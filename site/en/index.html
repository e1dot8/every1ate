Our approach

psst… highlighted words are clickable

We believe a brand only truly exists once people make it their own.

So we start with the right ingredients: what already makes a brand unique, its products, its story, its community, its codes, its world. We also watch what moves around it, and we never put the same seasoning on every brand.

Trends interest us when they serve the project. Otherwise, we go the other way. The right recipe connects the brand with people, and we carry it from the idea all the way to reality.

Our expertise

  • Understand what makes the brand unique and turn it into a clear direction. Then come up with the concepts that bring it to life: marketing ideas, campaigns, formats, talking points.

    For example: a brand that wants to reach a new generation without losing what makes it valuable. We define what it needs to say, then create the concept that sets it up.

  • Turn strategy into images: a universe, codes, a few rules, and an eye on every production so everything stays consistent.

    For example: a brand launching a collection, or an artist preparing a release. We build a recognisable visual universe that holds for the whole project.

  • Imagine, design and produce your advertising campaigns and social media content, with a format built for each channel.

    For example: a product launch. We create the campaign, produce it, then roll it out as content for every platform.

  • Already have the idea? We make it. Crew, shoot, post-production: pure production for brands, artists and agencies.

    For example: an agency that needs a crew to shoot its campaign, or an artist preparing a music video.

  • Bring the brand to life IRL: activations, launches, pop-ups, meet-ups. Moments designed to be lived on site and told before, during and after.

    For example: a brand or a store that wants to turn a launch into an event. We design the moment on site, then tell it online so those who missed it want to come next time.

Our method

Five stages, one team from the first chat to the final report. And at every stage, something concrete in your hands.

same method for everyone, only the seasoning changes

  1. The recipe

    Strategy & concept

    We listen and analyse the brand and its community. You get: the angle, the concept and the action plan.

  2. The prep

    Art direction & design

    Visual universe, casting, scouting, schedule: everything is ready before the shoot. You get: the approved art direction and the timeline.

  3. The cooking

    Production & execution

    Film shoot, photo shoot, event: our own crew on the ground. You get: live follow-up, no middlemen.

  4. The plating

    Post-production & adaptations

    Editing, grading, graphics, motion. You get: finished content, adapted for every channel.

  5. The service

    Delivery & reporting

    We deliver, then measure the results. You get: clear reporting on what worked and what we adjust.
